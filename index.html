Meet the Development Team

Centralized Educational Support Platform

In the midst of South Africa's education crisis, where the National Senior Certificate (NSC) pass rates are hitting record highs while overall Mathematics performance continues to drop, targeted intervention is more critical than ever. EduBridge simplifies tutoring management, tracks learner progress, and replaces manual spreadsheets with one definitive operational dashboard. Built for ClassRoom Buddies Organisation to turn administrative chaos into measured impact.
